CIN Mobile — Responsive Refresh Preview

Upload this ZIP as a new deployment to your existing crypto-net Cloudflare Pages project. Open https://crypto-net.pages.dev online; close the installed app and reopen it online if an older version appears.

The default chart is BTC/USD. Enter a ticker or select a coin in Market screen; USD spot availability and token identity depend on the provider and are not guaranteed. Public market candles come from the production CIN API. A validated Kraken public trade from the experimental test stream can update the selected USD price. It is drawn as a distinct dotted segment only when the historical chart is also from Kraken and the exact market matches. If providers differ or chart quality is limited, the live price is labeled separately and is not plotted onto unrelated candles. When no recent trade is available the app returns to closed-candle data.

The latest closed-candle chart is saved locally for at most 15 minutes to improve reopen speed. Saved data is explicitly labeled Cached and always refreshed; its age and any failed refresh are shown. A saved value is not called live. Previously received news, screening and indicator results can remain visible in memory during refresh for up to 10 minutes, labeled updating or last known on failure. Data from a different selected market is never reused. The service worker caches only app files, not API responses.

The live stream uses the separate Render test service. For the longer bounded session, deploy feat/ccxt-smoother-stream-sessions there with CIN_CCXT_PREVIEW_ENABLED=1 and CIN_CCXT_STREAM_ENABLED=1, and allow the Pages origin through CORS. Sessions stop after at most 300 seconds or 1,200 events and have a 30-second cooldown. The app reconnects after cooldown while open; it pauses while hidden and resumes when visible. Browser and Android background execution can be suspended, so continuous hidden-app streaming is not promised. A free Render service may take a minute to wake. Resource use and provider availability must be checked on the test service.

Public market data only: no exchange account, wallet access or trade execution. The top-five evidence-gated shortlist, social sentiment and forecast probabilities remain unfinished. Validated with simulated cache restore, stale/error cases, selected-market handling and stream updates; this revision has not been visually verified on a Samsung device or against the live backend.
